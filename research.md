## 🧠 Research Interests

Here are the key areas I focus on in my research:

### 1. LLMs for High-Stakes Documents
I work on trustworthy generation and evaluation of legal and technical documents, especially patent claims, using large language models (LLMs).

### 2. Trustworthy & Domain-Specific NLP
I aim to build evaluation methods that reflect legal validity, structural rigor, and domain-specific constraints.

### 3. Patent Intelligence & Strategic Analysis
I analyze patent corpora using LLMs to uncover innovation trends, assess IP value, and support strategic business decisions.
